Truth before momentum

Certification is not authorization.

A course may teach a skill. A certificate may document learning. A license, employer, regulator, insurer, or professional scope may still determine whether and how someone can offer the work.

🔐

Verify authority

Identify the official body, employer, school, regulator, or professional whose rules apply.

🧪

Label the evidence

Separate researched facts, simulations, supervised practice, experiments, and proven results.

🧾

Account honestly

Record costs, sources, permissions, limitations, attribution, and what remains unknown.

The bullseye is chosen, not imposed

Fast and inexpensive can be an entry route. Your deeper destination can still be ambitious, premium, and built to last.
